To begin with, I would like to mention a couple of things:

- Of course, I have a valid driving licence (Mexican)
- I have been driving for the last 21 or 22 years of my life. I vaguely remember, but I am sure my dad started teaching me when I was 13 or 14. As of today (2026-08-18) I am 35 years old. 
- In my past life (back in Mexico), I was usually the "designated driver" mainly because (according to my friends and family.) I drive very well.
- I have already taught some people the basics of driving. 
- I have used a car in plenty of different types of roads in many different countrie

So you get the gist. I know how to operate a vehicle, I'd say I'm very responsible about it and I for one, can absolutely learn the rules quite fast. 

Now a bit of context, 

- Around 3 years ago, I moved from London, UK to Brussels, Belgium, and being based here, there is a specific process, inside Belgium, but outside Brussels, things look slightly different. This post is Bxl specific
- I'm originally from Mexico, so moving to Belgium didn't carry any of my migratory status from the UK (thanks Brexit)
- **The worst one**: According to the office for Mobility and Transport, Mexico **IS NOT** recognised in the [list of countries](https://mobilit.belgium.be/nl/weg/rijden/rijbewijzen/info-voor-gemeente/lijst-van-landen-en-regios-buiten-de-europese-unie) that can exchange their non-EU driving licence for a Belgian one.

Before throwing myself into a rant about how difficult I had it, I want to acknowledge I am in a privileged position and some other folks in process of integrating to their host countries have it much much harder.

## Process

So for the process. Here's the official `Bruxelles Mobilité` poster

![permisb.jpg](https://oschvr.s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/2026/08/24/permisb.jpg)

In the image (in French) the steps weren't very clear to me so having gone through the process, I can explain a bit more: 

- There are 2 test centers:
  - [_La Sécurité Automobile_](https://www.securiteautomobile.be) - Rue du Labeur 3/9, 1070 Anderlecht (mostly FR)
  - [_Autocontrole_](https://www.autocontrole.be) - Rue Colonel Bourg 118, 1140 Evere/Schaerbeek (mostly NL)

> **NOTE**: Choose the one that is closest to you. I don't want to say this but you will end up going multiple times. I choose La Securite Automobile, and even though it was the closest, I still have nightmares about it. Eneded up going around 9 times.
> Also if you're not in Brussels, and you chose one of this, you will have to do your ENTIRE process in the region, that is one of this. 

In the poster is not super clear but the process is divided in 5 stages

- **Examen Théorique**
- **Formation à la conduite**
- **Formation Premier Secours**
- **Examen Perception des risques**
- **Examen Practique**

### Examen Theorique

- It's a computer based multiple question exam, comprised of 50 questions.
- To pass you need **41/50**
- A `faute grave` (serious fault) will cost you **5 points**, whereas a normal `faute` is **1 point**
- This means that you can get **AT MOST** once `faute grave`
- After 2 exam failures, you need to go to an official `auto-école` and do a 12-hour theoretical course, which costs between €100 and €160 if in French/Dutch. In English you can expect to pay up to €200 
- After passing, you can request your provisional driving license.
- Each attempt costs **19€**

So this was probably the most difficult part of the whole process. See I started studing the `code de la route` with two fantastic mobile apps, [passetonpermis.be](https://passetonpermis.be) and [feuvert.be](https://www.feuvert.be/), I had to pay around 12€/M for like 6 months of passetonpermis.be and 25€/M for feuvert.be

**TOTAL: (12€ * 6) + 25€ = 97€**

For some reason, I didn't thought of studying in another language than English, and never tought any of it, unfortunately, when I arrived to the exam center, they asked in french which language did I wanted to do the test in, FR or NL. When I asked EN, they said that I had to wait for the translation module to be fixed, book again, this time notifying in advance and pay an extra 65€.

**NEW TOTAL: 97€ + 19€ = 116€**

So I said FR, being the beginner french speaker and feeling quite confident about my knowledge, I went in with a positive mindset. Failed it.
I quickly changed to FR and started all lessons again. Went in for the attempt **2** the 14/10/2025. Failed again.

**NEW TOTAL: 116€ + 19€ = 135€**

**NEW TOTAL: 255€ + 19€ = 274€**

And finally passed attempt **4** on the 11/05/2026. I remember being incredibly nervous as I really really didn't want to do another 3 day course. Passed it with **41/50** (_the minimum_)

**NEW TOTAL: 274€ + 19€ = 293€**

### Formation à la conduite

**MISTAKE 1:** After I got the paper saying I passed the theorical exam, I didn't go straight to the `commune` to get the license until the 14/12/2025.
It is only after you get your provisional driving license (pink card) that your `Formation à la conduite` period starts. At this point you have to decide which `parcours` or `route` you want.

1. 9 months - No driving courses - With guide
2. 6 months - 14h driving courses - With guide
3. 3 months - 20h driving courses  - No guide
4. No stage - 30h driving courses - No guide

> **NOTE**: If you chose any of the first two, you need to register your guides in the [BelDrive](https://beldrive.mobilit.fgov.be/) platform.

**MISTAKE 2:** Without really understanding the implications of these, and hearing that driving courses were incredibly expensive, I chose 1. Had I know how the driving lessons worked I would have definitely chosen 4

So for me

> 14/12/2025 + 9 months = 14/09/2026

So only after the 14/09/2026 I would be able to make the practical exams

Oof... anyways I did end up waiting a long time... and while I was waiting. I had to do do the first aid course.

### Formation Premier Secours

**GOOD NEWS**: This is no longer required, [since 10 days ago, it was removed from the process.](https://permispass.be/blog/premiers-secours-et-permis-a-bruxelles-la-formation-pser-supprimee-le-15-aout-2026). 

In essence you had to do an online course (now defunct) and then to the Red Cross Belgium, book a slot and go to the city center for 1 hour of course. They give you a certificate that you need to take to the examination center.

### Driving Courses

Problem was I didn't have my permit yet... And at this point I realised I would have to wait until September 2026.

I read somewhere that you can reduce your stage by doing driving lessons with an `auto école`.

So to make it 

> 14/12/2025 + 6 months = 14/06/2026

I would need **14h** of driving lessons. So that is what I did.

I went to [`Auto école Européenne`](https://www.aee.be) and guess how much was the **14h** pack... **1200€**

So in retrospectibve, I think it made sense to do the *30h*, in my case. The cost of the **30h** is **2280€**, BUUUT, I would not have had to wait AT ALL for the practical exam stage.

And personally, I couldn't wait longer to make some progress (I really wanted to be able to travel), I forced the **14h** course to reduce my wait time from 9 to 6 months

**NEW TOTAL: 293€ + 1200€ = 1493€**

I'm not going to be timid about how the driving lessons (in my case, experienced driver), were a total waste of time.

### Examen Perception des risques

- It's a computer based multiple question exam, comprised of 5 questions with 10 "risks".
- To pass you need to detect **6/10** risks
- It's in the same place than in theorical
- Costs 17€

I took it on 13/05/2026 and passed it the first time with **8/10** risks detected

**NEW TOTAL: 1493€ + 17€ = 1510€**

### Examen Practique

I did an initial [research](https://passetonpermis.be/blog/deroulement-examen-pratique-permis-b) on how it looked like. 

- It's a practical exam, on the car
- In total 40min, of those ~32min driving and the rest are questions about the car and the manouvers
- You start and end in the same center as in the theorical
- If you did a `route` with a guide (`9` or `6` months), you can use your own car
- Costs 46€

## Steps I Had to Take

1. **Download study apps** - [passetonpermis.be](https://passetonpermis.be) and/or [feuvert.be](https://www.feuvert.be/). Study in the language you'll take the exam in (FR or NL, not EN).
2. **Book Examen Théorique** - 50 questions, need 41/50. Costs ~€19. Book at [La Sécurité Automobile](https://www.securiteautomobile.be) or [Autocontrole](https://www.autocontrole.be).
3. **If you fail twice → do the 12h theoretical course** at an `auto-école` (~€120–€200). Mandatory before attempt 3+.
4. **Pass the Examen Théorique.**
5. **Go immediately to the `commune`** to request your provisional driving license (pink card). Choose your `parcours`:
   - 9 months, no lessons, with guide
   - 6 months, 14h lessons, with guide
   - 3 months, 20h lessons, no guide
   - No stage, 30h lessons, no guide
   > The sooner you get the pink card, the sooner your waiting period starts. Don't wait.
6. **If you chose route with a guide** - register them on [BelDrive](https://beldrive.mobilit.fgov.be/).
7. **~~Formation Premier Secours~~** - _Removed since 15/08/2026. No longer required._
8. **Do your driving lessons** (if your chosen `parcours` requires them). Budget ~€1200 for 14h, ~€2280 for 30h.
9. **Book and pass Examen Perception des risques** - computer-based, 5 questions with 10 risks, need 6/10. Costs ~€17.
10. **Book Examen Pratique** - contact your `auto-école` for availability (don't trust the center's counter, they'll say _N_ months). Budget ~€160 for their slot + €46 exam fee.
11. **Pass Examen Pratique** - 40 min total, ~32 min driving. Be theatrical: exaggerate head checks, stop fully at STOP signs, stay under the speed limit.
12. **Take the result paper to your `commune` immediately.** They keep your provisional license. Get an email confirmation.
13. **Return to the `commune` after ~1 week** to pick up your `Permis B`.
