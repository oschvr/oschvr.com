Some of you are already using [Immich](https://immich.app) as your self-hosting photo solution, but for those who aren't, I seriously can't recommend it enough.

Last year, I realised that my photo and video collection, was one of my most precious possessions, if not the most! 

And at the time I was using Google Photos... While the service was absolutely great (if you have an Android), I stopped and though about the following:

- I was locked to Google's ecosystem (as I said, you need an Android to have the full experience)
- I had to pay a Google One's Premium subscription (2Tb) which would set me back ~100€/y
- I constantly felt paranoid of the idea of having Google train their AI models with my personal photos (after seeing this [tweet/x post](https://x.com/ProtonDrive/status/2003546423510642721))
- And their "trust me bro" [response](https://www.forbes.com/sites/zakdoffman/2025/12/27/new-google-photos-warning-issued-for-all-15-billion-users/) denying it.

So I decided I needed to leave Google Photos.  

Enter Immich.
